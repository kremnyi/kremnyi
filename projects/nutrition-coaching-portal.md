# Nutrition coaching / trainer portal

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/nutrition-coaching-portal](https://kremnyi.com/projects/nutrition-coaching-portal).

Portal for trainers and clients covering recipes, measurements, questionnaires, shopping lists, and admin flows.

## Delivery challenge

Calculation-heavy nutrition logic had to stay correct across three currencies and an affiliate program layered on top.

## My role

Managed delivery across trainer/admin/client interfaces, calculation-heavy workflows, validation fixes, multi-currency billing in GBP, EUR, and USD, ThriveCart and Mailchimp integrations, an end-to-end affiliate program — landing pages, link management, invitation flows — and production releases.

## What shipped

- Trainer and client portal: recipes, measurements, questionnaires, billing
- Nutrition calculations kept correct across GBP, EUR, USD
- Full affiliate program, from landing pages to invitation flows
- ThriveCart and Mailchimp integrations
- Shopping lists and trainer calendars
