# Data automation & lead-gen systems

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/lead-gen-data-automation](https://kremnyi.com/projects/lead-gen-data-automation).

Data platforms and scraper operations for B2B lead generation, enrichment, deduplication, and reporting.

## Delivery challenge

Scrapers had to keep 7M+ records usable through target-site changes, proxy failures, migrations, and CRM syncs.

## My role

Coordinated scraper stability, data quality fixes, AWS/server migrations, database updates, client handoffs, and reporting workflows.

## What shipped

- Pipelines that scraped 7M+ records and processed 1M+ B2B leads
- CRM enrichment and deduplication, synced to Pipedrive
- Looker Studio reporting
- Survived server migrations and repeated client handoffs
- Source-account and blocklist operations so the fleet survived site and access changes

Python, Django, AWS, nginx, Docker, SQL, web scraping, database sync, CSV exports, Pipedrive, Looker Studio
