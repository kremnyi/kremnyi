# Dating & events Telegram platform

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/telegram-dating-events-platform](https://kremnyi.com/projects/telegram-dating-events-platform).

Dating and local-events product built as a Telegram bot with a full Telegram WebApp (Mini App) client and a companion web app.

## Delivery challenge

The bot-first MVP had to become a full Telegram Mini App without losing creator, event, and subscription flows.

## My role

Managed delivery from bot-first MVP to the Telegram WebApp port: creator and user role flows, event attendance and matching journeys, venue management, subscription logic, and production infrastructure.

## What shipped

- Telegram dating/events platform grown from bot MVP to Mini App
- Geolocation event search and venue management
- Subscriptions and attendance flows
- EN/DE localization
- Matching and creator/attendee flows kept through the Mini App port

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 2,000+ hours • 230+ tasks • 8 contributors • 7 departments

Team mix: 3 backend, 3 frontend, 1 UI/UX, 1 PM/BA.

**First MVP:** 2022

**Customer market:** Estonia

## Technology

Python, Telegram Bot API, Telegram WebApp, ReactJS, WebSockets, geolocation services
