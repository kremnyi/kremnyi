# Expense & receipt digitization app

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/expense-receipt-digitization-app](https://kremnyi.com/projects/expense-receipt-digitization-app).

Finance app for the Baltic market: receipt capture, digitization, cost reports, and VAT handling for business users on iOS and Android.

## Delivery challenge

Receipt digitization for three markets meant releases tied to regulatory VAT schedules that could not slip.

## My role

Managed the mobile release train: localization across four languages, VAT logic changes, receipt digitization flows, store submissions, and GitHub-based handoffs to the client's team.

## What shipped

- Expense app for iOS/Android across three Baltic markets
- Receipt capture and digitization flows
- VAT-rule updates shipped on regulatory schedule
- Four-language localization, recurring store releases

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 85+ tasks • 7 contributors • 6 departments

Team mix: 2 iOS, 2 Android, 2 UI/UX, 1 PM/BA.

**First MVP:** 2024

**Customer market:** Finland & Estonia

## Delivery evolution

Built through 2023, store launch 2024.

Swift, Kotlin, iOS, Android, App Store, Google Play, GitHub
