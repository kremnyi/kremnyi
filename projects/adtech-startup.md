# AdTech startup

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/adtech-startup](https://kremnyi.com/projects/adtech-startup).

Advertiser-influencer marketplace for mobile app acquisition campaigns.

## Delivery challenge

Attribution across web and native mobile required first-party SDKs built alongside third-party tracker integrations.

## My role

Managed roadmap, advertiser/influencer interfaces, and releases across web and mobile teams — including the attribution SDKs shipped from v0 to production alongside AppsFlyer and Adjust integrations.

## What shipped

- AdTech platform spanning advertiser, influencer, and admin interfaces
- First-party iOS/Android attribution SDKs, v0 to production
- AppsFlyer and Adjust integrations
- Campaign budgets, balances, and payouts for advertiser/influencer settlements
- Influencer referrals tracked across 8 social platforms: Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Facebook, YouTube, Twitch, Tumblr, and Twitter
- In-app currency with a USD exchange rate, influencer cash-outs, and cash-out limits
- Referral, install, and weekly bonuses with a leaderboard

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 2,500+ hours • 350+ tasks • 15 contributors • 10 departments

Team mix: 3 backend, 3 frontend, 3 QA, 2 Android, 1 iOS, 1 UI/UX, 1 DevOps, 1 PM/BA.

**First MVP:** 2020

**Customer market:** UAE

## Technology

Swift, Kotlin, Java, iOS, Android, REST APIs, mobile attribution SDKs, AppsFlyer, Adjust, Intercom, push notifications, Google Play Install Referrer, PayPal, Twilio, Firebase
