# Medical ERP

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/medical-erp](https://kremnyi.com/projects/medical-erp).

Large B2B delivery from concept through scale for a medical ERP product.

## Delivery challenge

A medical distributor scaling across 4 countries needed invoicing, approvals, and regional sales-rep permissions working together from day one.

## My role

Led delivery across discovery, business analysis, SOW definition, effort estimation, planning, sprint breakdown, stakeholder communication, and cross-functional execution. Product core: proforma-to-invoice-to-payment pipeline, role-based permissions for regional sales reps, threshold-based purchase approvals, PDF document generation, and multi-currency support. Coordinated production database recovery from backups with DevOps.

## What shipped

- Medical ERP taken from concept to scale across 4 countries
- Country admin accounts for Rwanda, Burundi, Kenya, and DR Congo under one master account, with prices in RWF, BIF, KSh, or USD
- Proforma-to-invoice-to-payment flow with approvals and multi-currency
- Role-based permissions across 5 roles: superadmin, regional manager, sales rep, credit manager, and clinic customer
- Recurring weekly, monthly, or yearly product subscriptions for clinics
- PDF document generation, including VAT/TIN tax invoices
- Backoffice for catalog, customers, orders, and statements on the 8,000+ product operation

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 4,000+ hours • 550+ tasks • 17 contributors • 8 departments

Team mix: 5 backend, 6 frontend, 3 QA, 1 UI/UX, 1 DevOps, 1 PM/BA.

**Engagement:** 4-year engagement (2019–2024)

**First MVP:** 2019

**Customer market:** Austria

## Delivery evolution

2019 MVP, build through 2021, heaviest year 2022.

## Outcome

Client grew to ~50 employees, 8,000+ products, 1,500+ B2B customers, and multi-million-dollar investment across 4 countries.
