# B2B project-management SaaS

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/b2b-project-management-saas](https://kremnyi.com/projects/b2b-project-management-saas).

US-based SaaS product for workspaces, task boards, dashboards, notifications, and support workflows.

## Delivery challenge

Subscriptions, per-seat billing, and paid support had to evolve without breaking existing accounts.

## My role

Managed delivery across user/admin workflows, monetization logic — Stripe subscriptions, pay-per-seat with proration, trials, and refund handling — a paid email-support-desk add-on on Postmark, AWS infrastructure, and client repository handoff.

## What shipped

- Project-management SaaS shipped continuously over 3.5 years
- Workspaces, boards, dashboards, notifications, 50+ features total
- Free tier plus three monetization models: subscriptions, pay-per-seat, paid support desk
- AWS infrastructure and repository handoff

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 2,000+ hours • 230+ tasks • 13 contributors • 8 departments

Team mix: 3 backend, 4 frontend, 2 UI/UX, 2 QA, 1 DevOps, 1 PM/BA.

**Engagement:** 3.5-year product lifecycle

**First MVP:** 2021

**Customer market:** USA

## Technology

Python, Django, ReactJS, NextJS, AWS, REST APIs, Stripe, Postmark, email integrations
