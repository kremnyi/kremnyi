# Fitness tracking iOS/Android app

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/fitness-tracking-app](https://kremnyi.com/projects/fitness-tracking-app).

Mobile product delivery for an iOS and Android fitness app with Bluetooth hardware integration.

## Delivery challenge

Live workout tracking over Bluetooth hardware had to prove reliable on both platforms before consumer features could land.

## My role

Coordinated the whole project across mobile, backend, design, QA, and release work — from hardware de-risking with standalone BLE test apps to live WebSocket workout tracking, server-side logging, secure photo storage, and 15 production updates.

## What shipped

- iOS and Android fitness app, built through 15 production updates
- Live workout tracking over Bluetooth hardware for 9,000+ users
- Standalone BLE test apps to de-risk hardware before integration
- Server-side logging and secure photo storage
- Workout routines, categories, timers, and charts once live tracking was stable

## Delivery scale

**Published scale:** 3,000+ hours • 200+ tasks • 12 contributors • 15 updates

Team mix: 2 Android, 2 iOS, 2 backend, 2 QA, 2 UI/UX, 1 DevOps, 1 PM/BA.

**First MVP:** 2023

**Customer market:** Israel

## Technology

iOS, Android, BLE, WebSockets, App Store, Google Play, push notifications, mobile release pipelines
