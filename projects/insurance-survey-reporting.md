# Insurance surveys & report automation

> Published at [kremnyi.com/projects/insurance-survey-reporting](https://kremnyi.com/projects/insurance-survey-reporting).

Insurance surveys where an answer can hide whole sections, then generate a PDF from what remains.

## Delivery challenge

Answers could hide entire sections of a survey. Templates, prefilled responses, photos, and report layouts had to stay consistent as questions changed.

## My role

Defined question types, section visibility, image order, and how template answers copy into new reports. Took PDF and layout fixes through production updates.

## What shipped

Production survey app with reusable templates, prefilled answers, photos, and PDF reports. A broker/insurer portal stayed in discovery against budget.

**First MVP:** 2023

**Customer market:** Finland

## Technology

Django, REST APIs, PDF generation
